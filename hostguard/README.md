# hostguard

Pure host-admission decisions over injected telemetry (REQUIREMENTS §14.1–14.2).

- `Observe` samples memory available, **swap total**, PSI **full** avg10/avg60 for I/O and memory, load per core. Missing values stay `Unknown`; they are never substituted (no "some" fallback). Unreadable memory available (or `/proc/meminfo`) is such a gap, not an error: the hard breaker proceeds and the gap is recorded (FAE-HOST-004). Only an unsupported platform is an error.
- `Admit` / `AdmitResidentTurn`: hard breaker (FAE-HOST-003; no-swap floor when swap total is 0 or unreadable), pane and memory reservation, soft thresholds (FAE-HOST-002). A value exactly at a soft threshold proceeds.
- `Hold` (`hold.go`) is the hysteresis state. It performs no I/O and reads no clock.
  - `NewPressureHold`: entered by a soft-pressure reading. It is lifted only by two readings strictly below every soft threshold, at least `Window` (30 s) apart (FAE-HOST-005). A reading at or above a threshold while the hold is in force keeps the hold and restarts the chain.
  - `NewIOGate`: always in force for host-heavy commands. It needs I/O full avg10 strictly below `IOGate` (5.0) on two readings at least 30 s apart (FAE-HOST-010). Exactly 5.0 does not qualify, unlike FAE-HOST-002. After the gate passes, its chain is retained, so a later command passes on one fresh clear reading while the chain stays unbroken.
  - Stricter than the text: consecutive chain readings more than `MaxGap` (60 s) apart restart the chain, so an old reading never vouches for an unobserved interval.
  - Unreadable relevant telemetry proceeds with `Gap` set and leaves the chain unchanged (FAE-HOST-004).
  - Each reading records avg10/avg60 and the direction (rising/falling/steady/unknown) relative to the previous reading of the same hold.
- `Wait` is bounded back-off: a first reading plus `Checks` (10) re-checks `Interval` (3 s) apart, with a cancellable sleep on the injected `Clock`. It stops on `OutcomeProceed` or `OutcomeStop` (the hard breaker, used by callers). When the bound runs out it returns the last step: `OutcomeWaiting` (the second reading is still due) or `OutcomeRefused` (pressure is proven).
- `CanLiftPressureHold` (`hold_guard.go`) is the Gobra-verified two-reading predicate.

Daemon wiring is in `daemon/live_admission.go`. No wait holds a scarce resource it does not need:
- Agent sessions (AgentTurn, Review, including Prophet and loop turns) are not heavy ops: they take no heavy-op unit. They lease their role slot first, then back off on host pressure, panes at the limit and the per-pane memory reservation while holding only that slot. A Herdr (`herdr-core`) session reserves its pane in the shared pane ledger; a Direct session reserves its memory in the host-wide heavy ledger (`scheduler.ReserveLaunch`), where every launch of any instance still in its 180 s warm-up counts as pending (FAE-HOST-006). A new resident pane passes the same hold and back-off.
- Leased operations (every other primitive) back off on host pressure before the lease request. Host-heavy primitives (HostCommand, VcsCommit, Integrate) and daemon I/O maintenance (plane backup, replication, unpin, retention, cleanup, review workspaces, via `AcquireHostIO`) also pass the I/O gate there (`daemon/pressure_guard.go`). After the lease is granted, one fresh reading is taken; it never backs off while holding the lease.
- Panes at the limit and the memory reservation back off like soft pressure (FAE-HOST-002, -006); the hard breaker and configuration refusals stop at once. A hard refusal raises one `host-admission` alert per hard-pressure episode (FAE-HOST-003).
- Exhausted waits return a deferred `AdmissionRefusal` (`IOPressure`, `IOPressureHold`, `HostPressure`, `HostPressureHold`, `PaneLimit`, `MemoryReservation`). The engine parks the work and retries it; hold state persists across retries.
- A refused daemon-internal operation (`AcquireHostIO`) has no task to park, so its refusal is its own `host-admission-refused` instance event, written before its line (FAE-OBS-006).

Host-heavy commands outside the drive share one admission, `controller.HeavyAdmission`: `fae gate run`, landing, the controller roll's package build (`controller.BuildGated`, which also runs `go build -p 2` with TMPDIR/GOTMPDIR on disk under the deployment directory) and the `fae heavy-op run` broker. Each reading applies the hard breaker (an immediate refusal, reported as an alert line and through the caller's hook: landing raises the daemon's alert, `gate run` and `heavy-op run` spool it to the instance's `<run>/host-alerts`, which the daemon raises each observe round), the soft pressure hold, the landing floor (12 GiB; controller operations only) and the two-reading I/O gate; only then is the lease requested, and one reading under the granted lease must still be clear (pressure releases the lease and waits again). Controller- and operator-class waits (landing, `gate run`, the roll build, and `heavy-op run --class controller|operator`) are bounded by `MaxWait` (30 min; `heavy-op run`'s `--max-wait`) and report infrastructure; only a role-class `heavy-op run`, which every role shim's run is, backs off at most `Checks` re-checks (`HostChecks`) and refuses (`TestHeavyOpControllerClassWaitsPastTheRoleBound`). A wait emits one alert line per completed slice (default 1 min; `heavy-op run` sets the slice to its whole `--max-wait`, so the pool's own waiting event is a lease wait's one line). The reading that starts a hold (soft pressure, the landing floor, or I/O pressure at the gate) is recorded at once, however short the wait, and `gate lifted` records the reading that lifts the pressure hold or the I/O gate; the routine two-reading I/O window, whose readings are clear, adds no line. Every line that reports a reading (the first held reading, a slice line, including one for a reading under the granted lease, a timeout, a hard refusal, and `gate lifted`) carries memory available, swap total, load per core and the PSI full avg10 and avg60 of I/O and memory, each with its rising/falling/steady/unknown direction, with the same keys as the daemon's `host admission reading` line (FAE-HOST-005; `TestHeavyAdmissionPressureWaitRecordsAvg60AndDirection`, `TestHeavyAdmissionEveryRecordedReadingCarriesAvg60AndDirection`, `TestHeavyAdmissionRecordsAHoldShorterThanASlice`, and for `heavy-op run` `TestHeavyOpRecordsTheReadingsItWaitsOn`). The readings between the first and the next slice line, or the lift, are not recorded on this path. Under the default policy a role-class broker that meets soft pressure refuses at its tenth re-check before the hold can lift (the lift needs two clear readings 30 s apart after the first clear one), so its record is the first held reading and the timeout's. `gate run` and `heavy-op run` write their lines to standard error in the supervision log's form (FAE-OBS-001): an RFC 3339 UTC timestamp, `instance=` and `component=gate-run` or `component=heavy-op` before the text (`TestGateRunLinesCarryTimeInstanceAndComponent`, `TestHeavyOpLinesCarryTimeInstanceAndComponent`); they are not written to the instance's log file. Landing writes its lines to the daemon's supervision log as `component=admission`, and audits each line as a `landing-admission-wait` row whose outcome is `waiting`, `lifted`, `refused` (the hard breaker) or `timeout` (`TestLandingAdmissionAuditsEachLineWithItsOutcome`). Every lease access advances the ledger with the observed liveness of its owners (`scheduler.OwnerCache`), so a proven-dead owner's entry is reclaimed by whichever instance or command touches the ledger next (FAE-HOST-031). An unset ledger is the host-stable `~/.fae/host-ledger` (`scheduler.DefaultHostLedger`), shared by every instance of the user whatever its `--home`.
